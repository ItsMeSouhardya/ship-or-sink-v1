# Life Ruiner: Bad Decision Simulator

> *An interactive game where every choice makes your life worse… in the most entertaining way possible.*


## 🎮 Overview

**Life Ruiner** is a chaotic, interactive decision-based simulator that turns everyday situations into a spiral of terrible choices, regret, and dark humor.

Instead of helping users make better decisions, this app does the exact opposite - it **encourages the worst possible choices** and shows their hilarious (and painful) consequences over time.

---
## 🎯 Features

### Interactive Decision Engine
- Users enter a real-life scenario (e.g., *“Exam tomorrow, what should I do?”*)
- The system generates **3 terrible choices**
- Each choice leads to increasingly worse outcomes


### Consequence Timeline
Every decision unfolds across time:
- **Now** → Immediate result  
- **Tomorrow** → Short-term consequence  
- **1 Month Later** → Long-term chaos  


### Life Stats Dashboard
Track how badly your life is falling apart:
- Academics  
- Mental Stability  
- Sleep  
- Distraction  
- Regret  

Each decision dynamically impacts these stats.


### Chaos Meter
- Starts normal  
- Gradually escalates to:
  - Chaotic  
  - Catastrophic  

At maximum chaos, decisions become completely absurd.


### Overthinking Mode
Triggered after multiple bad decisions:
- Screen glitches
- Intrusive thoughts appear:
  - “Why did I do that?”
  - “Everyone else is ahead…”
  - “It’s over.”


### Alternate Universe Outcomes
Occasionally shows what *could have been*:
> “In another universe, you studied and topped the exam.”


### Narrator Personality
A built-in narrator reacts to your choices:
- Savage
- Sarcastic
- Fake motivational

Example:
> “Great choice. Absolutely terrible. Keep going.”


### Inactivity Nudge (Twist Feature)
If the user goes idle:

> “Wow. Even ruining your life requires effort-and you failed.”


### Reality Check Button
A last attempt to escape:

> “Fix my life”  
→ *“Too late. Should’ve thought earlier.”*
